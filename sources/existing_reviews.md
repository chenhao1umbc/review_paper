## Gap Analysis — What Our Paper Adds

**The specific intersection currently unoccupied in the review literature:**

A comprehensive review that simultaneously covers:
1. **Humanoid morphology restriction**: Only bipedal human-form robots — not mobile delivery robots, exoskeletons, surgical arms, or social companion robots (Pepper/NAO)
2. **Full clinical spectrum**: Social/companion roles, rehabilitation, hospital logistics, teleoperated clinical procedures, patient communication
3. **2023–2026 generation of foundation-model-enabled humanoids**: Figure 02, Unitree G1/H1, Boston Dynamics Atlas, Fourier GR-1, Sanctuary Phoenix, Apptronik Apollo, Tesla Optimus — none appear in any existing review
4. **LLM/VLM/VLA integration as the enabling AI layer**: The paradigm shift that distinguishes current humanoids from earlier Pepper/NAO-era platforms
5. **Clinically-oriented framework**: TRL assessment, regulatory analysis, deployment barriers — from a medical perspective

**Closest existing works and why they fall short:**
- The 2025 PMC scoping review (Cunha et al.) is humanoid-specific but covers only 27 studies with a Nov 2023 cutoff — it entirely misses the 2024–2026 LLM-humanoid convergence
- The 2025 Information Fusion survey (Liu et al.) covers embodied AI in healthcare comprehensively but is not restricted to humanoids and does not address the humanoid-specific hardware landscape
- The 2026 MDPI Robotics foundation models review covers LLMs in mobile service robots but is not humanoid-specific and treats healthcare as one of many verticals
- No existing review combines humanoid morphology + modern foundation models + clinical deployment analysis

**Novelty claim (refined):**
"While several reviews have examined robots in healthcare broadly and one scoping review has specifically addressed humanoid robots (with a 2023 search cutoff), no comprehensive review to date synthesizes the current landscape of foundation-model-enabled humanoid robots through a clinical deployment lens. This paper fills that gap."

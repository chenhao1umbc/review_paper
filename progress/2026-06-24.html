Global Figure Requirements

Style and Layout Rules

  • Figure text should be larger and visually close to the caption font size.
  • Across all paper figures, use consistent font size, color style, formatting, alignment, and whitespace logic.
  • Use fewer visual treatments where possible: fewer formats, fewer colors, fewer competing styles.
  • Figures should feel logically organized, extremely symmetric, and comfortable for scholarly reviewers to read.
  • A figure should not look like a second large paragraph block on the page. The visual area and caption should have clear hierarchy.

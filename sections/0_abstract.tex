\begin{abstract}
Humanoid robots capable of bimanual manipulation and natural language 
instruction are commercially available, yet no full-size bipedal humanoid 
holds Food and Drug Administration (FDA) clearance or Conformité 
Européenne (CE) marking for any clinical application. This survey 
systematically reviews humanoid robot applications in healthcare settings 
from 2022 to 2026. We synthesize evidence across 25 humanoid platforms, the
open-source software ecosystem, and four regulatory frameworks, and we
systematically screen the clinical literature to identify the complete set of
studies that place a full-size bipedal humanoid in a clinical task. We assign
ISO~16290:2013 Technology Readiness Level (TRL) criteria to five clinical
domains and find that all five remain at TRL~3--4. The three roles nearest TRL~5 
are teleoperated clinical assistant, rehabilitation demonstrator, and 
eldercare companion. The key binding barrier is the lack of accepted bipedal 
gait safety standards for patient-proximate environments.
\end{abstract}
